Factory operating system · ~12 people · Bootstrapped · Ghent

Factry

18 months → 6 client implementation time

The situation

Factry builds software that runs on factory floors. Their previous client implementation had taken 18 months and nearly broke the team. A new client was coming. The technology wasn't the problem. How decisions were made and how work moved through the team was.

On the factory floor

I sat with the machine operators who would use the system, built rough prototypes and walked them back through those same operators before a line of production code was written. That removed weeks of rework.

In the team

The daily meeting had become an hour-long conversation between one developer and the founder while seven people waited. We took the founder out of it. Five one-hour meetings a week became fifteen minutes each.

The result

The next comparable client implementation took 6 months. Not because of a new framework or tool. Decisions happened faster, the right people were involved at the right time, and the team stopped waiting.

Real estate marketplace · CIB Vlaanderen · First product hire

Spotto

10,000 → 200,000 monthly visitors, over two years

The situation

CIB, the federation of Belgian real estate agents, had tried to build this platform before. External developers were shipping code, but nothing checked whether it matched what users needed. Features got built because someone important asked for them.

Evidence instead of opinions

I added the one thing that was missing: regular contact with the people using the product. Conversations, surveys, data. Decisions stopped being about who had the strongest opinion.

Shorter waits

External developers were emailing questions and waiting days for answers from the business side. The product manager joined their Slack. Response times went from days to minutes.

The result

The platform grew from 10,000 to over 200,000 monthly visitors in two years. Not because we moved faster. Because we stopped building the wrong things.
