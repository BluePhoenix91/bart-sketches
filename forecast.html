What you leave with

One page for your CEO or board

The summary is written to be forwarded as it is. "The forecast says 28%" is something you can say in a board meeting. "I have a bad feeling" is not.

The chance on each date

A probability, the realistic date and the gap, for up to five dates you've promised.

The reasons

What is stuck, what grew after the date was set, where the pace changed. About the system, never about people.

What to do this week

Specific steps per date. When nothing needs doing, the forecast says so.

A second run after four weeks

The same numbers again, with a short note on what moved and what didn't.

An hour with me

We read the forecast together, so you hear what I'd do first and can push back.

How it goes

  1. Scoping call

    30 minutes, free. We agree which dates are in, and I confirm the price and the day you get the forecast.

  2. You connect your tracker

    The same read-only connection as the Scan. A file export works too, if your security team prefers that.

  3. One working session

    45 to 60 minutes with you, and a team lead if useful. What your statuses mean, which dates were promised, and to whom.

  4. I build the model

    I map how work really moves through your team and run 10,000 simulations per date.

  5. We read it together

    One hour. You get the forecast as a PDF right after.

  6. Four weeks later

    I run the numbers again and send a short note on what moved.

Is this for you?

Yes, when

  • You have two or more teams, or several dates running at once.
  • You answer for dates you can no longer check ticket by ticket.
  • Your tracker has at least twelve weeks of history, however messy.

Start somewhere else when

  • You have one team and one date. Run the free Scan, then book a read-out.
  • There is no usable tracker history. Start with the first two days of the Realign.
  • The question is what to build, not when it lands.
