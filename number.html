Yours will have your project, your date and your number.

What it is

A forecast, built from how fast your team actually finished work in the last twelve weeks. A computer played the remaining work forward 10,000 times. The percentage is how often it was done by the date.

It is not an estimate, not an opinion and not a performance review.

What a low number means

A low number rarely means a slow team. It usually means the date was fixed before anyone could know, or the work grew after the date was set.

The person who sent it didn't cause it. They measured it.

Three things you can change

Time

Move the date to the realistic one, and tell the client now instead of later.

Scope

Keep the date and ship less by then. The card says how much less.

Pace

Remove what slows the team down. Adding people rarely works in time.

Three questions to ask back

  1. Which of the three would you change?
  2. What is stuck right now, and what would unstick it?
  3. How do our other dates look?
