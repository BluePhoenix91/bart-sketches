Questions people ask first

Are you in our office every day?

No. The two days of phase 1, yes. In phase 2 I'm there for the moments that matter, fewer every week. Phase 3 is handover.

Can't we just run a retrospective?

You can, and you should. But a retro surfaces what the team already knows. I don't work here, so I see what nobody sees because they're too close.

What if the two days turn out to be enough?

Then that's the whole engagement. The setup is yours, and the four-week check-in tells us whether it held.
