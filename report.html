How it goes

  1. Scoping call

    30 minutes, free. We agree which commitments are in, and I confirm the price and the day you get the report.

  2. You connect your tracker

    The same read-only connection as the Scan. A file export works too, if your security team prefers that.

  3. One working session

    45 to 60 minutes with you, and a team lead if useful. What your statuses mean, which dates were promised, and to whom.

  4. I build the model

    I map how work really moves through your team and run 10,000 simulations per commitment.

  5. We read it together

    One hour. You get the report as a PDF right after.

  6. Four weeks later

    I run the numbers again and send a short note on what moved.
