Project 1 of 4

Customer portal redesign

Started October 2026 · 8 developers · 110 items left

Why

  • The team finished 38% less work over the past six weeks. Normally they finish 8 to 12 items a week. Recently that dropped to 5 to 7. The amount of work in progress hasn't changed, so the slowdown comes from work getting stuck, not from the team being stretched.
  • Seven items are stuck. Five have been open longer than similar work usually takes. Three haven't moved at all in over two weeks.
  • At the current pace the 110 remaining items take 16 to 22 weeks. At the normal pace, 9 to 14. March 31 is 11 weeks away, so the date only holds if the team is back at its normal pace within days. That is what the 28% says.
  • 22 items were added since kickoff, 20% more than planned. Some may be fixes for earlier work.

What to do

  1. This week, take the three items that haven't moved in two weeks. Find what blocks each one and give each a single owner.
  2. Stop adding work to this project until the stuck items are cleared.
  3. Go through the 22 added items with the team lead. Separate new requirements from rework. If most of it is rework, the cause sits earlier in the process.
  4. Don't add people. This team delivers steadily when nothing is in the way. More people on a blocked team raises the cost, not the pace.

Method

How these numbers were made

The data

Workflow data from Jira Cloud, January 2026 to January 2027: when items moved between statuses, when they were created and resolved, blocker flags. No descriptions, comments or code.

The chance

Each project was simulated 10,000 times. Every run draws weekly results from the team's own history and plays the remaining work forward. The chance is the share of runs that finished by the committed date.

The realistic date

The median: half of the runs finished earlier, half later. It is not a guarantee.

The limits

This is how things stand today. If scope changes, blockers clear or people move, the numbers move with them. The model can't see what has never happened to these teams.
