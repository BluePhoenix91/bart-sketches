What this number says, and what it doesn't

It says

  • How fast your team finished work in the last twelve weeks, fast weeks and slow weeks included.
  • What happens when that pace meets the 24 items still open, simulated 10,000 times.
  • That 86 in 100 of those simulations finished by March 15.

It doesn't say

  • That the date is safe. 14 in 100 runs still missed it.
  • What happens when work is added. The number is the best case: it assumes the 24 items stay as they are and your team keeps its recent pace. Most teams add work along the way. The card shows the room you have: 4 more items.
  • How your other dates stand. They count on the same team.
  • Anything about things that have never happened to your team before.
