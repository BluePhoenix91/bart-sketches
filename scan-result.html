What this number says, and what it doesn't

It says

  • How fast your team finished work in the last twelve weeks, fast weeks and slow weeks included.
  • What happens when that pace meets the 67 items still open, simulated 10,000 times.
  • That 34 in 100 of those simulations finished by March 15.
  • That 85 in 100 finished by May 24. That is the realistic date.

It doesn't say

  • Why the chance is 34%. That is in how your team works, and the number can't see it.
  • What happens when work is added. The number is the best case: it assumes the 67 items stay as they are and your team keeps its recent pace. Most teams add work along the way. Then the real date is later than May 24.
  • How your other dates stand. They count on the same team.
  • Anything about things that have never happened to your team before.
