What is used, and what isn't

Used

  • When an item changed status
  • When it was created and resolved
  • What type of item it is

Not used

  • Titles and descriptions
  • Comments and attachments
  • Who did the work

Why a chance and not a date

Same estimate. Days or weeks.

I checked 880 software tasks with the same kind of work, the same estimate and the same process. Half were done within three days. One in twenty took longer than four weeks.

There was no better number to find. Your own history has the chance for every date.
