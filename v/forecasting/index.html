How the forecast is made

  1. It reads what your team finished.

    The last twelve weeks from Jira or Azure DevOps: when items changed status and when they were resolved. Not estimates, not story points, and not who did the work.

  2. It plays your remaining work forward 10,000 times.

    Every run draws on those twelve weeks, the fast ones and the slow ones.

  3. It counts the runs that make your date.

    If 3,400 of the 10,000 runs finish by March 15, your chance is 34%. The realistic date is the one that 8,500 runs made.

Before you connect your tracker

Do you need our estimates or story points?

No. The forecast runs on what your team finished and when. Nobody has to estimate anything.

Why not a Jira plugin?

If you already run one and trust it, keep it. This is for answering one question without installing or configuring anything. It takes three minutes and gives one answer, in a form you can forward to someone who never opens Jira. If the answer is bad, a person can find out why.

How is this different from the dashboard we already have?

A dashboard shows how work is moving today. The forecast tells you where a date you already promised ends up.

How do I know the number is right?

It is your team's own twelve weeks, played forward. The method and the data behind it are public. It is also the best case: it assumes no work gets added and your team keeps its recent pace. Run it again next month and see whether the date is getting closer.

Is the free forecast enough on its own?

If you only need to know whether you'll make one date, yes, and it's free to run again. The Audit is for the why: what holds the date back, what to change first, and how the dates behind it look.

What do you read from our tracker?

Status changes, created and resolved dates, and issue types. No titles, descriptions, comments or attachments, and not who did the work. The connection is read-only. Your data stays in memory while the forecast runs. The result is kept 90 days, so you can share it.

We don't use Jira or Azure DevOps.

The forecast connects to Jira Cloud and Azure DevOps. On Linear or another tracker? Tell me which one and we'll find a way.

Will you contact me afterwards?

No. There is no account and no sales call. If you want to talk, you book it.

What if our tracker is a mess?

The forecast still runs. It counts an item as done on the day your tracker marks it resolved. If your releases happen later than that, so does your real date. Checking what "done" means in your team is part of the Audit.
