Why a chance and not a date

Why tasks take very different amounts of time

I checked 880 software tasks with the same kind of work, the same estimate and the same process. Half were done within three days. One in twenty took longer than four weeks.

It's public data, so you can check it. There was no better estimate to find. Your own history has the chance for every date.

Where does a number like 34% come from? Take the example at the top: 67 items, promised for March 15. I don't ask the team for a better estimate. I take what they finished in the last twelve weeks, fast weeks and slow ones, and play the 67 items forward 10,000 times. 3,400 of those runs are done by March 15. That's the 34%. The realistic date is the one 8,500 runs made.

Before you start

Do you need our estimates or story points?

No. The forecast uses what your team finished and when, not what anyone estimated.

How is this different from the dashboard we already have?

A dashboard shows how work moves through your team today. The forecast tells you where you end up, for a date you've already promised to someone.

How do I know the number is right?

It isn't an opinion. It's your team's own last twelve weeks, played forward. The method and the data behind it are public, and running it again next month shows whether the date is getting closer.

Is the free Scan enough on its own?

If you can still move the date or cut scope, yes. You get the chance, the realistic date and how much of each closes the gap, and it's free to run again next month. If you want a second opinion on one date first, the Scan Review is one hour with me and a one-page note for €750 excluding VAT. When neither can move, the Delivery Audit starts from the same calculation and finds what is holding the date back, like finished work that waits days for a review.

What do you read from our tracker?

Status changes, created and resolved dates, and issue types. No titles, descriptions, comments or attachments, and not who did the work. The connection is read-only. Your data is only held in memory while the forecast runs. The result is kept 90 days, so you can share it. What is used, and what isn't →

We don't use Jira or Azure DevOps.

The free Scan connects to Jira Cloud and Azure DevOps. Working with another tracker like Linear? Tell me which one and we can find a solution. Working in Notion, Excel or on a whiteboard? Start with the Delivery Setup.

Will you contact me afterwards?

No. No account, and nobody calls you. If you want to talk, you book it.

What if our tracker is a mess?

The Scan still runs. It counts an item as done on the day your tracker marks it resolved. If your releases come later than that, so does your real date. Checking what "done" means in your team is part of the Delivery Audit.
