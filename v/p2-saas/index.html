How it works: three steps to your number

1. Connect your tracker read-only

Jira Cloud or Azure DevOps. The Scan reads status changes, created and resolved dates and issue types. It cannot change, create or delete anything.

2. Pick the project and the date

Choose the project that worries you and enter the date you promised, or the one you are about to promise. It takes about three minutes in total.

3. Get the chance and what closes the gap

Your team's last twelve weeks are played forward 10,000 times. You see the chance, the realistic date, and how much later or how much less closes the gap.

There is no estimate or story point to enter. The forecast uses what your team finished and when. The method and the data behind it are public.

Where the number comes from, and why it is a chance

I checked 880 software tasks with the same kind of work, the same estimate and the same process. Half were done within three days. One in twenty took longer than four weeks.

It is public data, so you can check it. There was no better estimate to find, which is why the Scan gives a chance and not a single date.

Where does a number like 34% come from? Take the example above: 67 items, promised for March 15. I do not ask the team for a better estimate. I take what they finished in the last twelve weeks, fast weeks and slow ones, and play the 67 items forward 10,000 times. 3,400 of those runs are done by March 15. That is the 34%.

Questions people ask before they start

Why would I pay €149 a month when the Scan is free?

You would not need to, if running the Scan yourself each month is enough. The Monthly Forecast is for when you want it done for you, kept as a history longer than 90 days, and explained in a short note.

Is the free Scan enough on its own?

If you can still move the date or cut scope, yes. You get the chance, the realistic date and how much of each closes the gap. When neither can move, the Delivery Audit starts from the same calculation and finds what is holding the date back, like finished work that waits days for a review.

Do you need our estimates or story points?

No. The forecast uses what your team finished and when, not what anyone estimated.

How is this different from the dashboard we already have?

A dashboard shows how work moves through your team today. The forecast tells you where you end up, for a date you have already promised to someone.

How do I know the number is right?

It is not an opinion. It is your team's own last twelve weeks, played forward. The method and the data behind it are public, and running it again next month shows whether the date is getting closer.

What do you read from our tracker?

Status changes, created and resolved dates, and issue types. No titles, descriptions, comments or attachments, and not who did the work. The connection is read-only. Your data is only held in memory while the forecast runs. The result is kept 90 days, so you can share it.

We don't use Jira or Azure DevOps.

The free Scan connects to Jira Cloud and Azure DevOps. Working with another tracker like Linear? Tell me which one and we can find a solution. Working in Notion, Excel or on a whiteboard? Start with the Delivery Setup.

What if our tracker is a mess?

The Scan still runs. It counts an item as done on the day your tracker marks it resolved. If your releases come later than that, so does your real date. Checking what "done" means in your team is part of the Delivery Audit.

Will you contact me afterwards?

No. No account, and nobody calls you. If you want to talk, you book it.

Someone else has to approve the Audit. What do I tell them?

The Audit is a fixed €4,500, excluding VAT, with a written report and a second measurement four weeks later. You can read a sample audit before you decide, and forward it as it is.
